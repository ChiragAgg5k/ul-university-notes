# Gang of Four (GoF)
CS6451 — Advanced Software Design | 2026/7 SEM1 | Class 01

## Lecture clarification
The recommended-reading slide lists “Gamma et al.” for *Design Patterns: Elements of Reusable Object-Oriented Software*. Its four authors are collectively known as the **Gang of Four (GoF)**:

- **Erich Gamma**
- **Richard Helm**
- **Ralph Johnson**
- **John Vlissides**

The nickname refers specifically to these four authors—not all the authors or books on the reading list. The professor's remembered reference to “the gang” likely referred to this group.

## Why the name matters
Their book catalogs **23 object-oriented design patterns**, grouped into three categories:

| Category | Focus | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Creational | How objects are created | Factory Method, Abstract Factory, Builder, Prototype, Singleton |
| Structural | How classes and objects are composed | Adapter, Decorator, Facade, Composite |
| Behavioral | How objects interact and responsibilities are distributed | Observer, Strategy, State, Command |

The examples above are not the full list of 23 patterns.

**Memory aid:** “GoF patterns” means the patterns from this book, not every software design pattern.

## Source and context
Book: Gamma, Helm, Johnson, and Vlissides, *Design Patterns: Elements of Reusable Object-Oriented Software*, Addison-Wesley (listed as 1995 in the lecture slides).
