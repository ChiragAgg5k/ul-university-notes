Three questions, in order

What is the aim? How many conditions? Same or different subjects? Then ask whether a trend was predicted.

A difference between conditions
├── 2 conditions
│     ├── same subjects ........ Wilcoxon
│     └── different subjects ... Mann-Whitney
└── more than 2 conditions
      ├── same subjects ........ Friedman        trend: Page's L
      └── different subjects ... Kruskal-Wallis  trend: Jonckheere
A relationship between two variables ... Spearman's rho
Counts in categories ................... Chi-square

Parametric tests need interval data, a normal distribution and equal variance. If one fails, the test is non-parametric. A two-tailed test is harder to pass than a one-tailed one, because allowing either direction doubles the probability of a chance result.
