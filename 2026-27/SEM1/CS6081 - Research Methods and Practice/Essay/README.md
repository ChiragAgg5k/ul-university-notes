## Brief

A critical review of the state of the art in an SE area. Jim recommends picking your dissertation area, because supervisors may read it.

1. Reference at least 10 articles, and at least 8 of them must be journal or conference papers. Use no more than 3 from any one institution or researcher. Tangential material can go in a separate bibliography
2. Summarise them as a cohesive whole, across papers rather than one paper at a time. Start from a journal review article
3. Find a significant gap, flaw or link between themes that points to a research question
4. Write a preliminary research plan of 5 paragraphs or less
5. Optional, for the top grade: run a very small pilot study and report its design and observations
6. Declare your AI use transparently
