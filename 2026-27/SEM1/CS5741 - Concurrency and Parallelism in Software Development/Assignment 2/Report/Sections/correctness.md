A local spread test starts with one burning corner in a 7 × 7 forest of trees. With ignition probability one and growth probability zero, exactly eight neighbours burn after one step, including the diagonally opposite corner. The original corner is empty. Checking the subsequent rings tests whether ignition has travelled more than one cell in a single step. The serial checks also verified count conservation and repeatability with a fixed seed.

The first parallel comparison checked the printed counts against serial output in 504 cases per variant. Two grids can have the same counts with fire in different places. A second checker compared the full grid for sizes 7, 37 and 100, two seeds, six worker counts (1, 2, 3, 4, 8 and 16), six deterministic configurations and every step count from 1 to 40. All 8,640 grids matched. These cases include uneven partitions and more workers than rows. The shared generator is excluded from trajectory comparisons because draw order depends on scheduling.
