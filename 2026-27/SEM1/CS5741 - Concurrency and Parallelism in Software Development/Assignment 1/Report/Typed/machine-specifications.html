The M2 Pro has 8 performance cores and 4 efficiency cores, with no SMT (simultaneous multithreading). So there are 12 cores, and the 16-thread runs use 4 more threads than cores.

Speedup for n = 500m:

Threads2481216
Speedup1.91×3.68×6.20×7.76×7.83×

At 2 and 4 threads the speedup is close to ideal. From 8 threads on it is much smaller in comparison, and going from 12 to 16 threads adds almost nothing because there are only 12 cores.
