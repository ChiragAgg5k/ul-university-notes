Why do the endpoints have weight ½?

The area of one trapezoid strip is h × (left height + right height) / 2, so:

strip 1 = h/2 × (f(x0) + f(x1))
strip 2 = h/2 × (f(x1) + f(x2))
⋮

Every interior point is shared by two neighbouring strips, so its two halves combine into a full weight. Only the first and last points keep a weight of ½.
