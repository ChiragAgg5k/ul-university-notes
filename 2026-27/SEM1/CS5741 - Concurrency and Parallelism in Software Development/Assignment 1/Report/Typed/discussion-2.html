Effect of problem size

The program scales better with bigger problems. The overhead is fixed at about 1 ms, so there are no scaling issues from it.

Amdahl vs reality

Amdahl's law predicts a speedup of 13.8–15.9× at p = 16, but we measured 6.0–7.8×. This gap is mostly down to the hardware.

Evaluation of design choices
