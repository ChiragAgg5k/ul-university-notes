Amdahl's law gives the speedup as:

S(p) = 1 / (s + (1 − s) / p)

where s = serial (non-parallelisable) fraction, (1 − s) = parallelisable fraction, p = number of threads and S(p) = speedup. The maximum possible speedup is 1 / s.
